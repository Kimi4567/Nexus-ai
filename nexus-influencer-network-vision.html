01 — The Vision

الطرفين يستفيدوا

NEXUS currently serves brands. Adding a Creator side turns the platform into a two-sided network — brands find their perfect match, influencers manage their entire collab business, and the AI connects them based on real DNA alignment, not just follower counts.

🏢

For Brands

Current NEXUS users — upgraded

  • Browse creators AI-matched to their Brand Brain (industry, tone, audience)
  • One-click AI Brief Generator — adapts campaign strategy into influencer brief automatically
  • Filter by platform, follower range, engagement rate, niche, language
  • View DNA Match Score before reaching out — know fit before spending credits
  • Pipeline view: pending → accepted → active → completed collabs
  • ROI tracking per influencer — which creators drive real results
  • Brand Brain learns from collab outcomes — improves future matches
🎬

For Influencers & Creators

New free account type on NEXUS

  • Free Creator Account — sign up, connect platforms, get discovered
  • Smart Media Kit: auto-pulls stats from connected socials (followers, ER, top content)
  • Set collaboration rates and availability per platform
  • Receive brand collaboration requests directly in-app
  • Use NEXUS AI tools to write captions, hooks, and content strategy for paid collabs
  • Build Creator Score over time — brands see trust signals
  • Portfolio — showcase completed campaigns and brand partnerships
🧬 The Unfair Advantage

DNA-Based Brand ↔ Creator Matching

Every other influencer platform matches on numbers — followers, reach, category. NEXUS can do something none of them can: match based on Brand Brain DNA. We compare the brand's tone, values, audience, pain points, and positioning against the creator's actual content style, audience demographics, and niche authority. The result is a Match Score — a real compatibility signal, not a vanity metric.
