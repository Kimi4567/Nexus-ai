# Property Photo Film demo asset manifest

## Scope and truth boundary

- Experiment: source-locked property photo film.
- Property: Villa Tugendhat, Brno.
- Purpose: internal product-quality demonstration only.
- This is not a live property listing, brokerage instruction, availability claim, or offer for sale.
- The film may describe only what is visibly present in the selected photographs. It must not state a price, address-level listing location, floor area, room count, availability, view, amenity, delivery, return, or performance claim.
- The six selected photographs depict the same property. The source pages identify the exterior files as Villa Tugendhat and the interior files as interior photographs of Villa Tugendhat.
