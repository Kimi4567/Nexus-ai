# STRATEGY-RUNTIME-TRUTH6 — Strategy Workbench Planning Copy

## Scope

This follow-up keeps the global `/strategy` workbench aligned with the current product contract:

- Strategy output is review material before execution.
- Paid work is a planning brief until budget, conversion path, tracking, account readiness, and explicit approval are confirmed.
- The campaign page remains the place for the full strategy brief and execution decisions.
- Content Hub remains the source of truth for post readiness and media state.

## Runtime copy cleanup

The old paid surface label used campaign wording:

- `Paid Campaign Plan`
- `خطة الحملات المدفوعة`

That language can imply a runnable campaign plan. The surface now uses:

- `Paid Planning Brief`
- `بريف التخطيط المدفوع`

The paid copy also states that planning stays review-only until required readiness inputs and explicit approval are confirmed.

## Readiness wording cleanup

The old completed-strategy headline said:

- `Your strategy is ready to continue.`
- `استراتيجيتك جاهزة للمتابعة.`

That was too broad. The page now says:

- `Strategy direction is available for review.`
- `اتجاه الاستراتيجية متاح للمراجعة.`

The readiness helper also avoids `run planning update` language and uses cost-review wording instead.

## Non-goals

This change does not:

- generate strategy
- generate content
- generate visuals or images
- approve, schedule, publish, or manually publish
- activate Autopilot
- change billing, credits, schema, dashboard, campaign output, SocialPost, Media, or GeneratedVisual behavior
- change paid launch/platform push behavior
- change engine/rebuild behavior
