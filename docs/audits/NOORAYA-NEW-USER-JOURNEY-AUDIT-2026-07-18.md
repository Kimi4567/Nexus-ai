Date: 2026-07-18
Environment: `codex/100-readiness-phase-1` preview
Mode: observation first; no product fixes until the journey is complete.

## Test brand contract

- Brand: NOORAYA Abayas (fictional test brand)
- Market: UAE
- Business model: DTC e-commerce
- Audience: women aged 24–40 seeking modern modest fashion
- Offer: premium everyday abayas; no discounts, delivery promise, price, fabric claim, sustainability claim, return promise, or social proof may be invented.
- Initial objective: qualified product-page visits and completed purchases.
- Languages: Arabic-first with English support.
- Channels to evaluate: Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, and paid social only when the strategy supplies evidence and a measurable role.
- Human approval: required before scheduling, publishing, or spend.

## Final findings

The end-to-end observation reached Brand Brain → Full Strategy → paid quality review → 12 copy drafts → bulk copy approval → execution, publishing, performance, analytics, learning, operations, connections, billing, and Media Library. The journey stopped before a new NOORAYA asset could be uploaded because neither controlled browser surface exposes an authorized file-picker API; this is recorded as an audit-environment blocker, not a verified upload defect.

### Remediation completed in the local product build

- Registration now explains provider rejection of reserved/test-only email domains instead of claiming valid syntax is malformed (N-003).
- Starter and full Brand Brain now share Fashion & Apparel, support multi-select age ranges, treat channels as current **or planned**, allow no-channel brands, and route visual evidence to Media Library with explicit claim limits (N-009, N-011, N-013, N-014, N-017).
- Sales strategy readiness now requires a real conversion URL plus AOV and gross margin before a sales package is treated as ready (N-016, N-023, N-038).
- Strategy/content guards remove unsupported work, culture, collection, comfort, durability, and shopping-CTA claims; every reviewed strategy platform is required and content slots remain bound to the saved platform/format instead of silently remapping Pinterest (N-028–N-034, N-039, N-042–N-044).
- The 30/60/90 roadmap and exit gates are now visible on the Strategy Decision Desk; a missing roadmap is an explicit unmet promise (N-037).
- Content Hub recomputes all image **and video** media decisions, labels dates as proposed until scheduled, and refuses bulk approval when the current copy fails the quality/grounding review (N-045, N-048, N-049).
- The strategy confirmation shows strategy-only cost, cost through copy drafts, and a bounded full-production estimate before credits are spent (N-046, N-061, N-073).
- Navigation-only media actions can no longer be approved or written as completed approval evidence; Approvals and Calendar treat them as review-required navigation (N-051, N-074, N-075).
- Creative planner uses the real brand snapshot, supplies a platform/format-specific requirement for every post, distinguishes preview validation from production readiness, uses saved brand colors or one labeled neutral fallback, keeps interface/content language separate, and retains semantic page structure (N-056–N-060).
- Connections priorities come from the current campaign; Meta grouping and Google Ads account/manager access tiers are evaluated together and `NONE` cannot be described as execution-ready (N-067, N-068).
- Campaign portfolio exposes actual content/media/scheduled/published/failed workflow state while retaining the narrower “Strategy approved” status (N-069).
- Wallet checkout is fail-closed when Stripe tier prices do not match the commercial schedule (N-076).
- Future strategy, Sentinel, content-plan, and creative transactions can settle against the purchased artifact so Credit History can navigate to it. Legacy unlinked rows remain honestly labeled historical instead of receiving invented links (N-065, N-066).
- Workspace Reset copy now states that billing, credit/purchase ledger, and platform connections are account history preserved across brand journeys (N-004, N-052).

### Verification completed

- Full test suite: **316 test files / 2,399 tests passed**.
- TypeScript: **passed**.
- Production build: **passed**, including 123 static pages and all dynamic/API routes.
- Local browser: registration rejection copy rendered correctly in Arabic and no console warnings/errors were captured on that checked page.

### Still not proven by this journey

- A new NOORAYA image/video upload and product-specific media analysis were not completed because of the controlled file-picker limitation (N-062).
- No provider publish, paid campaign launch, real analytics, conversion, or approved learning proposal was executed. The product correctly kept those outcome surfaces empty; this remains external-pilot work, not evidence of 100% live readiness (N-063, N-064, N-070).
- The UI now keeps reset, onboarding, Brand Brain, Approvals, and Today in explicit in-progress/freshness states instead of rendering a false completion or empty result. End-to-end response time still requires production observability after deployment; it is not presented as solved by copy alone (N-005, N-008, N-019, N-051, N-071).
- Historical ledger rows without pricing versions/artifact IDs cannot be retroactively “fixed” without source evidence; only new transactions are guaranteed traceable.
- Human-quality differentiation of generated audiences/angles/copy must be re-tested with a newly generated, evidence-complete brand after these guards are deployed; deterministic safety is now stronger, but creative excellence is not proven by unit tests alone (N-026, N-027, N-035, N-036).
