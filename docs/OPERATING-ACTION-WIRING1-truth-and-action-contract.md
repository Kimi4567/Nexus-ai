# OPERATING-ACTION-WIRING1

## Product promise

NEXUS is an AI marketing operating system, not a collection of generators. It
should move a customer through one evidenced operating loop:

1. Brand Brain stores the current business context and reviewed signals.
2. Strategy turns that context into an explicit organic, paid-planning, or full
   decision document.
3. Campaigns coordinate workstreams and expose their current state.
4. Content Hub owns final post packages, post-linked media, and workflow status.
5. Creative Studio produces review assets and editable compositions; it does not
   silently attach or publish them.
6. Connections and Publish expose account, permission, readiness, and explicit
   execution boundaries.
7. Analytics receives real platform evidence. Only then may NEXUS describe
   performance patterns as learning.

## Surface ownership

| Surface | Owns | Does not own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Cross-system evidence, next best action, recent activity | Invented scores or unsupported performance claims |
| Campaigns | Portfolio and campaign command state | Final post editing |
| Content Hub | Final post package and linked-media source of truth | Asset-library management or silent publishing |
| Creative Studio | Creative production and review artifacts | Final SocialPost attachment without confirmation |
| Calendar | Saved scheduling records | Proof that a platform publish occurred |
| Publish | Connected-account readiness and explicit execution | Brand Brain learning |
| Analytics | Platform metrics and analytics-backed interpretation | Learning from approval, scheduling, or manual-publish workflow signals |

## Action contract

Every visible command must be exactly one of these:

- A real navigation link to an existing operating surface.
- A local UI control whose effect is immediate, reversible, and visible.
- A mutation action with explicit cost, effect, confirmation, loading, success,
  and failure states.
- A disabled command with a visible reason and the requirement that unlocks it.
- Read-only information styled as information, never as a clickable button.

Opening a page or adding a query parameter must never spend credits, generate
content, approve, schedule, publish, attach media, or mutate Brand Brain.

## Evidence contract

- Campaign, post, schedule, publish, account, and activity counts come from saved
  records.
- A missing metric renders as waiting or unavailable, never as a fabricated zero
  or a deterministic demo number.
- A real measured zero remains valid after an analytics payload contains a known
  platform metric.
- Approval, manual publish, scheduling, and selected variants are workflow or
  preference signals only.
- Performance learning requires a recognized numeric metric in `analyticsData`.
- Dashboard percentages must disclose their denominator or source. Arbitrary
  weighted health scores are not permitted.

## Organic and paid boundary

- Organic strategy may proceed from complete core brand context.
- Paid planning requires explicit budget, audience, offer, conversion
  destination, lead handling, platforms, and measurement assumptions.
- Paid execution additionally requires connected ad accounts, permissions,
  destination readiness, tracking readiness, approved creative, approved budget,
  and explicit customer confirmation.
- Planning readiness must never be labelled launch readiness.

## Trust acceptance criteria

1. No native button is inert or ambiguous.
2. No read-only preview invents engagement numbers.
3. No URL visit auto-triggers a paid or mutating action.
4. No performance-learning label appears without real analytics evidence.
5. Campaigns and Content Hub remain distinct and explain their ownership.
6. Every next action advances the strategy-led operating loop.
7. Browser QA confirms desktop/mobile layout, navigation, console health, and no
   unexpected mutation requests before release.
