# STRATEGY-OUTPUT-CONTRACT1 — Strategy generation truth contract

## Why this exists

Production QA after the billing status fix generated an organic strategy for a reset Brand Brain. The run succeeded, but the output exposed product-contract issues:

- The cost review promised a detailed first-30-day content calendar and generated posts.
- The strategy route currently creates a strategy review artifact only; Content Hub draft posts and saved calendars are generated separately.
- The model added Pinterest/blog execution even though the brand platforms were Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
- KPI targets included unsupported performance claims such as `Increase by 20%` and `Double current monthly requests`.
- Readiness checklist items could appear as `done` even when NEXUS had no proof that the operational setup was completed.

## Contract

Strategy generation may create:

- a roadmap for the selected horizon,
- first-window execution outline via `weeklyExecutionPlan`,
- content angle directions via `contentAnglesDetailed`,
- KPI directions that require a baseline,
- paid planning artifacts when the selected mode includes paid planning.

Strategy generation must not claim it created:

- saved Content Hub content plans,
- final SocialPost drafts or captions,
- scheduled calendar entries,
- platform/API publishing,
- final ad launch/spend/activation,
- analytics-backed learning without analytics data.

Strategy UI copy follows the same boundary. Language selection and cost-review
labels describe strategy output, execution outlines, and post directions. They
must not imply that the strategy run creates final content drafts, saved Content
Hub plans, or monthly scheduled post calendars.

## Platform scope

If Brand Brain provides selected platforms, those platforms are the execution boundary. The strategist may not add unsupported execution channels such as Pinterest, blog, LinkedIn, or website unless those were explicitly selected.

The post-process guard now:

- removes unsupported platforms from `channelMix`,
- rewrites unsupported `contentAnglesDetailed.platform`,
- filters `weeklyExecutionPlan.platforms`,
- keeps campaign creation sourced from selected platforms rather than model-generated channel mix when selected platforms exist.

## Readiness scope

Readiness checklist items generated by the model are review tasks, not verified operational state. The guard forces generated readiness items to `done: false` and softens known unsafe labels such as WhatsApp setup into confirmation tasks.

## KPI scope

User-provided numbers may be echoed only in their own factual context. A budget, margin, or price number must not unlock unrelated KPI claims. Unsupported performance percentages and multiplier wording such as `double monthly requests` are rewritten to baseline-needed targets.

## Proof scope

Client stories, customer stories, before/after transformations, testimonials, case studies, awards, reviews, guarantees, and performance proof require verified proof/assets. Without verified proof, output should frame them as proof or assets to collect.

## Deliberately not changed

- No schema changes.
- No billing or credit behavior changes.
- No SocialPost, Media, GeneratedVisual, campaign.aiOutput mutation outside future generation runs.
- No publishing, scheduling, approval, manual publish, Autopilot, paid launch, or platform push behavior changes.
- No PR #164 changes.
